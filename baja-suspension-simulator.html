Recess angle is measured from the longitudinal (Z) axis to the pivot-axis chord in the side (Y–Z) view. Knuckle angle is measured from the outboard (X) axis in top view, about the kingpin axis. Spindle length is the perpendicular lateral (X) distance between the wheel centre and the kingpin axis, taken at wheel-centre height (positive = WC outboard of the axis). Kingpin offset is the perpendicular longitudinal (Z) distance between the wheel centre and the kingpin axis, taken at wheel-centre height (positive = WC forward of the axis). Both are the static reference — see Mechanical Trail and Scrub Radius in Live Telemetry for the live, ground-projected, travel-dependent versions.

Setup & static angles flip / limits

Wheel-travel spec: BUMP 6in (152.4mm) · DROOP 4in (101.6mm).

Solved as a spatial 5-bar linkage (bump steer enabled): each wishbone rotates about its inboard pivot axis; the upright is carried between positions by aligning the old kingpin axis to the new one, and then rotated about the kingpin to perfectly satisfy the fixed tie-rod length constraint. KPI & caster read from the kingpin axis.

AI TUNING reads the live hardpoints and travel-table from the Simulator tab and checks them against general-purpose double-wishbone target ranges (camber gain, bump steer, roll-centre height, KPI/scrub, caster/trail). It proposes two alternative correction packages — Set 1 keeps wheel track and wheelbase exactly as-is, Set 2 is allowed to touch track/wheelbase too when that gives a cleaner fix. These are heuristic engineering guidelines, not a substitute for your own judgement or class rules — always sanity-check the resulting charts on the Simulator tab after pushing a change.
